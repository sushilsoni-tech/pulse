Fusion rules

how raw readings become events

Activity

Vibration σ over 2 s, step cadence and GPS speed score five states. A new state must hold for 1.5 s before it is reported.

Dead reckoning

When GPS is older than 3 s or worse than ±35 m, each step moves the position 0.72 m along the compass heading. Drift is reported when GPS returns.

Metal detection

|B| is compared with a 15 s adaptive baseline. More than 12 µT for 0.4 s is an anomaly. On iPhone, heading change the gyro cannot explain is used instead.

Compass trust

While walking, compass heading is checked against GPS course. A gap above 25° lowers heading trust and the awareness index.

Impact and fall

Free fall below 3 m/s² for 0.1 s, then a spike above 24 m/s². After 2.5 s, stillness means a possible fall; movement means a stumble.

Covered area

Light below 400 lux together with weak GPS reads as entering a covered area; above 1500 lux reads as open daylight.
